WEBSITE PROJECT PROPOSAL- KKK 4X4 OFF-ROAD WORLD
Organisation Overview: Name: KKK 4X4 OFF-ROAD WORLD KKK 4X4 OFF-ROAD WORLD is a fictional South African company that sells and install 4X4 off-road body kit and engine upgrades, for maximum engine performence, comfortability and reliability. The organisation aims to make vehicles that are capable of going off-road, and make them safe and enjoyable for people that are interested in modifying their vehicles to go off-road. Mission: To provide premium, affordable and reliable parts and upgrades that will boost and modify the vehicle to its maximum capabilities. Vision: To become the best company that provides premium parts and upgrades in South Africa. Target Audience: Young adults, Tourists and everyone who is interested in modifying their vehicles the way they want it to be modified.

Website Goals and Objectives The website will: • Increase awareness of the organisation and its services. • Allow customers to view parts and other services we provide. • Generate enquiries and customers. • Provide important information such as prices, locations and contact details. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Website visits, booking enquiries, completed bookings, contact-form submissions and social-media referrals.

Current Website Analysis The organisation currently has no professional website. Information is mainly shared through social media and for now the website is still under development, and it is making it difficult for customers to find complete information. The new website will provide a central, professional and easy-to-use platform.

Proposed Website Features and Functionality The website will include: • Homepage: Introduction, featured activities and clear calls-to-action. • About Us: Organisation history, mission and values. • Services: Detailed descriptions, prices and images. • Booking/Enquiry Form: Customers can request bookings. • Contact Page: Contact details, location and social-media links. • FAQ: Answers to common customer questions.

Design and User Experience The design will use an adventurous, modern and professional aesthetic. • Colour Scheme: Dark green, earthy brown, white and orange accents. • Typography: Poppins for headings and Open Sans for body text. • Layout: Clean, responsive and mobile-friendly. • User Experience: Simple navigation, readable content, accessible buttons and consistent page structure. • Wireframes: Low-fidelity wireframes will be created before development.

Technical Requirements The website will use HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript. It will be responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile devices. Reliable web hosting and a suitable domain name will be required.

Timeline and Milestones • Week 1: Research, requirements and wireframes. • Week 2: Visual design and content preparation. • Week 3: Website development and functionality. • Week 4: Testing, corrections and final presentation.

Budget Estimated costs: • Development: R 10,000 • Domain and hosting: R2,500 per year • Maintenance: R1,000 per year • Total first-year estimate: R7,500
